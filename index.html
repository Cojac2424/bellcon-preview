Connection matters.

BellCon is built to help inmates and their loved ones stay connected through the hardships of incarceration. Choose a local BellCon number near the institution and forward incoming calls to the destination allowed by your plan.

Why families use BellCon

Local virtual number included • Call forwarding included • Canadian long distance included • U.S. forwarding available with Premium • Change your forwarding destination from My Numbers.

The savings

More time talking. Less spent on calls.

The institution's telephone system can charge by the minute. BellCon's purpose is to provide a local-number option that can make regular communication more affordable for families.
